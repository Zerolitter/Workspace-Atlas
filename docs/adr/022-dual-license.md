# ADR-022: MIT or Apache-2.0 dual license

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Workspace Atlas is available under either the MIT license or Apache License
2.0, at the recipient's option.

## Rationale

The pairing is conventional in the Rust ecosystem, supports permissive reuse,
and lets recipients choose Apache-2.0's explicit patent grant when required.
The complete terms are in [`LICENSE-MIT`](../../LICENSE-MIT) and
[`LICENSE-APACHE`](../../LICENSE-APACHE).
